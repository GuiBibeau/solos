Accept `kind: "range"` on the Strategy contract. `RangeParamsSchema` names the Meteora DLMM pool, the PositionV2 account, the replacement width, the outside-bin debounce, the daily recenter budget, and slippage for the withdrawal and the deposit. A confirmed `remove_liquidity` may record quoted token A and token B proceeds. `carry` stays named until its issue ships.
